# Fertility and Ageing Transitions

Track fertility, cohort structure, dependency, partnership, migration, and demographic projections as processes rather than only population outcomes.

## Key variables

| Variable | Unit | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Total fertility rate | births_per_woman | annual | Do not substitute birth count |
| Age-specific fertility rate | births_per_woman | annual | Do not substitute for total fertility |
| Old-age dependency ratio | ratio | annual | Do not substitute life expectancy |
| Youth dependency ratio | ratio | annual | Do not substitute youth share |
| Net migration rate | per_1000 | annual | Do not substitute immigration alone |
| Median age | years | annual | Do not substitute mean age |

## Paired distinctions

- Fertility rate vs birth count
- Population ageing vs life expectancy
- Immigration vs emigration vs net migration
- Youth vs old-age dependency
- Population growth vs physical growth
- Total vs completed fertility

## Standing question

> Are we tracking the demographic processes that shape populations, or only the population outcomes?
