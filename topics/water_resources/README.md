# Water Resources and Sanitation

Track water availability, access, quality, use, sanitation, stress, transboundary flows, and groundwater depletion. Water infrastructure is not equivalent to actual access or use.

## Key variables

| Variable | Unit | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Renewable water resources | cubic_metres_per_person | annual | Availability is not access |
| Safe drinking water access | percent | annual | Infrastructure is not actual access |
| Sanitation coverage | percent | annual | Coverage is not quality |
| Water stress index | index | annual | Stress is not scarcity |
| Groundwater depletion | cubic_metres | annual | Surface water is not groundwater |
| Water quality index | index | annual | One contaminant is not overall quality |

## Paired distinctions

- Availability vs access
- Infrastructure coverage vs actual use
- Sanitation access vs quality
- Surface vs groundwater
- Water stress vs scarcity

## Standing question

> Are we tracking the water that sustains life, or only the water that is visible?
