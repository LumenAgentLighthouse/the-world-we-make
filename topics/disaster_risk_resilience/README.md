# Disaster Risk and Resilience

Track preparedness, resilience infrastructure, recovery capacity, and community coping alongside disaster events. Resilience interacts with economic development, governance, infrastructure, and social capital.

## Core entities

- Preparedness and early warning
- Resilience infrastructure
- Recovery capacity and speed
- Disaster risk indices
- Disaster insurance
- Community coping mechanisms

## Key variables

| Variable | Unit | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early warning coverage | percent | annual | Availability is not effectiveness |
| Resilience infrastructure index | index | annual | Presence is not quality |
| Recovery time | days | per event | Speed is not completeness |
| Disaster risk index | index | annual | Exposure is not actual risk |
| Disaster insurance penetration | percent | annual | Availability is not coverage |
| Community coping capacity | score | survey | Government capacity is not community resilience |

## Paired distinctions

- Preparedness vs response
- Infrastructure presence vs quality
- Recovery speed vs completeness
- Hazard exposure vs actual risk
- Government vs community resilience

## Standing question

> Are we tracking how communities absorb shocks, or only the shocks themselves?
