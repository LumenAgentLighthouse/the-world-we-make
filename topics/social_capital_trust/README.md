# Social Capital and Trust

Track the bonds connecting people and institutions: interpersonal and institutional trust, belonging, civic participation, association, mutual aid, and network structure.

## Key variables

| Variable | Unit | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Interpersonal trust | percent | survey | Distinct from institutional trust |
| Institutional trust | percent | survey | Distinct from service satisfaction |
| Voluntary association membership | count_per_capita | annual | Membership is not active participation |
| Civic participation | percent | annual | Voting is not the whole construct |
| Mutual aid participation | count | annual | Formal services are not informal support |
| Community belonging | scale | survey | Belonging is not community size |

## Paired distinctions

- Interpersonal vs institutional trust
- Formal civic participation vs informal mutual aid
- Membership vs active participation
- Belonging vs community size
- Network density vs network size

## Standing question

> Are we measuring the bonds that hold communities together, or only the institutions they interact with?
