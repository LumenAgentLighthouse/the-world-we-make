# Financial Inclusion and Exclusion

Track access to and actual use of banking, credit, payments, insurance, and informal finance. Inclusion and exclusion must remain distinct because formal access does not imply meaningful participation.

## Key variables

| Variable | Unit | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Account ownership | percent | survey | Ownership is not active use |
| Digital payment usage | percent | annual | Availability is not use |
| Credit access | percent | survey | Supply is not demand |
| Insurance penetration | percent | annual | Policy count is not coverage depth |
| Informal financial participation | percent | survey | Formal exclusion is not informal inactivity |
| Financial literacy index | score | survey | Education is not financial capability |

## Paired distinctions

- Account ownership vs usage
- Formal credit access vs credit demand
- Insurance penetration vs insurance depth
- Payment availability vs payment use
- Financial literacy vs financial inclusion

## Standing question

> Are we measuring who can participate in the financial system, or only who has a bank account?
