# Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services

Biodiversity loss and ecosystem degradation are longitudinal phenomena requiring tracking across decades and centuries. Ecological data must sit alongside social data because ecological changes interact with human populations, economies, and settlements.

## Core entities

- Species populations (by taxon, region, time)
- Habitat extent and condition
- Ecosystem services
- Biodiversity indices
- Threat and conservation status
- Invasive species spread
- Genetic diversity

## Key variables

| Variable | Unit | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Species population count | count | annual | Do not substitute occurrence for abundance |
| Habitat extent | area | decennial | Do not substitute extent for condition |
| Species richness | species_count | annual | Do not substitute richness for evenness |
| IUCN threat status | category | annual | Do not substitute status for population trend |
| Invasive species count | count | annual | Do not substitute detection for establishment |

## Paired distinctions

- Species abundance vs occurrence
- Habitat extent vs condition
- Protected coverage vs effectiveness
- Species richness vs ecosystem health
- Native vs invasive species

## Data quality

Use `KNOWN`, `PARTIALLY_KNOWN`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, or `UNMEASURABLE`.
