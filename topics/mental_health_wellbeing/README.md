# Mental Health and Subjective Wellbeing

Track psychological experience alongside physical health. Mental health prevalence, diagnosis, treatment, suicide/self-harm, wellbeing, and life satisfaction are related but non-equivalent phenomena.

## Key variables

| Variable | Unit | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Depression prevalence | percent | annual | Diagnosis is not prevalence |
| Anxiety prevalence | percent | annual | Treatment is not prevalence |
| Life satisfaction | scale | annual | Self-reported |
| Suicide rate | per_100000 | annual | Attempts are not completed suicide |
| Mental health service access | percent | annual | Availability is not access |
| Psychological wellbeing index | index | annual | Composite measure |

## Paired distinctions

- Prevalence vs diagnosis
- Subjective vs objective wellbeing
- Treatment access vs utilisation
- Suicide attempts vs completed suicide
- Psychological wellbeing vs life satisfaction

## Standing question

> Are we capturing the psychological dimension of human experience, or only the physical?
