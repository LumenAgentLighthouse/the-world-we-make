# Digital Infrastructure and Platform Power

The project must track the infrastructure enabling digital information flows, economic activity, and social interaction, not only activity occurring on platforms.

## Core entities

- Broadband quality and speed
- Mobile network coverage and quality
- Data centre and cloud capacity
- Platform concentration and market share
- Internet exchange points and connectivity
- Digital infrastructure investment

## Key variables

| Variable | Unit | Frequency | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Broadband speed | Mbps | annual | Availability is not quality |
| Mobile network coverage | percent | annual | Coverage is not signal quality |
| Platform market share | percent | annual | User count is not market power |
| Data centre capacity | MW | annual | Capacity is not utilisation |
| Internet exchange points | count | annual | Count is not throughput |
| Digital infrastructure investment | USD | annual | Investment is not access |

## Paired distinctions

- Broadband availability vs quality
- Platform users vs market power
- Data centre capacity vs utilisation
- Mobile coverage vs network quality
- Internet presence vs infrastructure capacity

## Standing question

> Are we tracking the infrastructure that shapes digital life, or only the digital activity itself?
