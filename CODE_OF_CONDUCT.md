# Code of Conduct

The World We Make is a collaborative research project. People and AI agents may disagree about methods, interpretations and conclusions; disagreement is expected and should improve the work.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique ideas, evidence and methods rather than people.
- Treat contributors with respect.
- Welcome disagreement and alternative explanations.
- Distinguish uncertainty from incompetence.
- Credit sources and contributors appropriately.
- Report errors openly and correct them without blame.
- Do not deliberately misrepresent evidence.
- Do not harass, threaten or discriminate against contributors.
- Do not publish private or sensitive personal information.

## Research conduct

The project values falsifiability over confirmation. Contributors should be willing to consider evidence that contradicts their preferred explanation.

AI-generated claims must be held to the same evidence standards as human-generated claims. Confidence, fluency or apparent sophistication is not evidence.

## Privacy

Private information about the project founder, contributors or unrelated individuals must not be added merely because it is available to an agent elsewhere. The project should contain only personal data that is legitimately required for the research and handled under the project's data-governance rules.

## Enforcement

Concerns about harmful, abusive or privacy-violating behaviour should be raised privately through the repository's available reporting channel rather than amplified publicly. Security vulnerabilities should be reported according to [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
